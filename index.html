Our Story

A neighborhood table for three great cuisines.

Nova Asian Fusion brings together the best of Japanese sushi with Chinese and Thai cooking. Chef and owner Peter breathes a fresh new appeal into the idea of "fusion" — here it means the signature dishes of three dynamic cuisines, under one roof on Jericho Turnpike, all at approachable prices.

Try us and you'll be convinced. Our recipes are authentic and time-tested, and the ingredients are always fresh. The result is a menu that bursts with flavor — from hand-cut sushi and signature rolls to wok-fired curries, hibachi, and our all-you-can-eat sushi.
