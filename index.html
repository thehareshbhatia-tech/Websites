Our Story

A neighborhood table for three great cuisines.

Nova began with a simple idea: the best of Japanese, Thai, and Chinese cooking deserves to live under one roof — without compromise and without pretense. Chef Peter trained at sushi counters in New York and Tokyo before opening Nova on Jericho Turnpike, where his team hand-cuts every piece of fish and stir-fries to order in a wok-fired open kitchen.

Whether you're here for a quick weekday lunch, an unhurried date night, or gathering the whole family for our all-you-can-eat sushi, you'll find the same thing on every plate: ingredients we'd serve our own family.
